# Pricing & Commercials

> **Important:** The example ranges below are preliminary hypotheses for selected software/digital-service offers only. They are not market averages, approved price lists, or prices for CCTV, equipment, cabling, physical installation, or third-party products. Validate all pricing using real quotes, cost, effort, and customer response.

## Early digital-service pricing hypotheses

| Offer | Initial test range | Qualification |
|---|---:|---|
| Limited business/technical audit | EGP 5,000–12,000 | Defined duration and deliverable |
| Small company website | EGP 20,000–45,000 | Content, integrations, e-commerce, and migration affect scope |
| Initial custom business-system MVP | EGP 40,000–100,000 | Only for a tightly bounded first release |
| Limited monthly care plan | EGP 5,000–15,000/month | Defined hours, response window, and exclusions |

These are unapproved test ranges and must not be published as official prices without validation. Complex systems, mobile apps, migration, urgent deadlines, international engagements, and broad integrations require separate estimates. Product and template prices remain undecided until the actual catalog, license, delivery, updates, and support are defined.

## IT equipment and installation quotation

Do not copy the digital-service ranges into physical IT/security jobs. Build each quote from:
- Site survey and requirements, including area/layout and customer constraints.
- A bill of materials: camera/device models, recorder/storage, networking, cable, accessories, power, and compatible components where applicable.
- Supplier quotation, availability, warranty, delivery cost, and quote validity.
- Labor/installation/commissioning and configuration.
- Consumables, cabling routes, access equipment, transport, and other site-specific costs.
- Testing, documentation, customer handover, and training if included.
- Post-install support, warranty boundaries, and recurring maintenance if selected.
- Tax treatment and payment milestones as advised/contracted.

Separate equipment, installation labor, third-party costs, and recurring support in the quote. Confirm whether a site visit is needed before issuing a binding price.

## Estimate any project

Include discovery/site assessment, design/planning, delivery labor, testing, handover/documentation, communication, risk/contingency, supplier dependencies, recurring costs, taxes, warranty/support obligations, and collection risk. Do not price only by coding hours or device cost.

## Proposal structure

1. Customer context and desired outcome.
2. Scope and itemized deliverables.
3. Exclusions, assumptions, and dependencies.
4. Milestones, timeline, and acceptance criteria.
5. Price, currency, taxes, and payment schedule.
6. Change-request process.
7. Equipment/software ownership, third-party fees, licensing, and IP where applicable.
8. Warranty, maintenance, support hours, and exclusions.
9. Quote validity and approval/signature process.

## Payment principles

Use an advance payment and milestone installments appropriate to the risk and workload. Exact percentages are deal-specific, not universal company policy, and must be written in the agreement. Confirm cleared funds before starting work that depends on them. For overseas clients, clarify currency, payment fees, exchange risk, transfer method, taxes, and timing.

## Review after every engagement

Compare estimate with actual time, equipment/vendor cost, unplanned work, collection delay, warranty/support load, margin, and customer value. Improve future quotes using evidence.
