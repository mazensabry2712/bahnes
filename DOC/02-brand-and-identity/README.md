# Brand & Identity

## Brand name and status

**BAHNES IT SOLUTIONS** is the working public-facing brand. Legal-name availability, domain, social handles, and trademark conflicts require separate checks; this document does not confirm registration or trademark ownership.

## Brand personality

- Premium, corporate, calm, and capable.
- Clear and practical rather than overly technical.
- Credible across both physical IT services and digital engineering.
- Confident without inflated promises.
- Consistent across the website, proposals, email, social profiles, service materials, and product interfaces.

## Working positioning

**Preferred direction:** “Integrated IT services, security systems, and digital solutions for businesses.”

This positioning is intended to fit both service lines:
1. **IT Infrastructure & Security:** CCTV, networks, business IT setup, and technical support.
2. **Software & Digital Solutions:** websites, applications, business systems, integrations, automation, and validated digital products.

Optional tagline to evaluate later: **“Technology that keeps your business moving.”** This and all other copy remain proposals until reviewed and approved; do not treat them as final or registered marks.

## Visual direction — proposal

- Primary: midnight/deep navy.
- Supporting: warm ivory or white and neutral gray.
- Accent: restrained mint/teal.
- Typography candidates: Manrope for Latin; IBM Plex Sans Arabic or a comparable readable Arabic family.
- Layout: strong hierarchy, coherent grid, deliberate whitespace, precise alignment, and clear calls to action.
- Visuals should balance equipment/network/site imagery with software interfaces and system diagrams; neither side should make the other feel like an unrelated company.

Proposed starting palette: Midnight Navy #101C35, Mint #55D6BE, Warm Ivory #F5F4EF, Steel Gray #667085. These are not final design tokens. Test contrast and legibility in both light and dark themes, Arabic and English, mobile layouts, proposal documents, and small-size logo use before finalizing.

## Logo brief

Develop a distinctive geometric B monogram suggesting structure, connection, and stability, paired with a clear BAHNES wordmark and smaller IT SOLUTIONS descriptor. Explore primary, compact, monochrome, light-background, and dark-background variants. Avoid generic camera/chip/cloud symbols, excessive gradients, glow, and fake 3D. Keep editable source files and export/spacing guidance once a design is approved.

## Brand voice

Prefer specific statements such as “We design, install, and maintain agreed business IT systems” or “We build and support business software” over generic claims such as “the number one technology company.”

Use plain language, verified capabilities, clear scope, consistent English/Arabic terminology, and truthful examples.

Avoid fake client logos, invented testimonials/results/certifications, guarantees without evidence, stock imagery presented as project proof, or listing every technology in every headline.
