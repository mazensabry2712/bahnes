# Brand & Identity

## Brand name

**BAHNES IT SOLUTIONS** is the working public-facing name. Confirm legal-name availability, domain availability, social handles, and trademark conflicts independently before investing heavily in the identity.

## Desired personality

- Premium and corporate.
- Clear, calm, capable, and practical.
- Business-focused rather than overly technical.
- Confident without exaggerated claims.
- Consistent across proposals, website, email, social profiles, and product interfaces.

## Positioning line

Working option: **“Business software and digital systems for growing businesses.”**

Working tagline option: **“Software that works as hard as your business.”**

Both are proposals, not final approved copy or registered marks.

## Visual direction (proposal)

- Primary: deep navy.
- Supporting: white and neutral gray.
- Accent: restrained mint/teal.
- Typography candidates: Inter or Manrope for Latin; IBM Plex Sans Arabic or a comparable readable Arabic family.
- Layout: strong hierarchy, generous spacing, consistent grids, restrained effects, and clear calls to action.

Do not finalize the palette, fonts, or logo until they are checked in real layouts, including Arabic, mobile screens, proposal documents, and small-size logo usage.

## Brand voice

Prefer specific statements such as “We build and maintain business systems” over unsupported claims such as “the number one digital transformation company.”

Use:
- Plain language and clear outcomes.
- Concrete scope and timelines only when supported.
- Honest case studies with permission and measurable results.
- Consistent English and Arabic terminology.

Avoid:
- Fake client logos or invented testimonials.
- Guaranteed growth or performance claims without evidence.
- Stock imagery presented as customer work.
- Listing every technology in every headline.

## Identity asset checklist

- [ ] Approved primary logo and compact mark.
- [ ] Light and dark logo variants.
- [ ] Favicon and social avatar.
- [ ] Color and typography tokens.
- [ ] Proposal and quotation template.
