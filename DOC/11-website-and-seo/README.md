# Company Website & SEO

## Primary website goal

Turn a relevant visitor into a qualified inquiry by explaining who the company helps, which problems it solves, how engagements work, and what a sensible next step looks like.

## Suggested information architecture

- Home
- Services
- Case Studies / Work
- About
- Insights / Articles
- Contact
- Privacy notice and other required legal pages, as advised

Start small. Add pages when there is enough credible content to help a real visitor.

## Homepage essentials

1. Clear positioning and primary call to action.
2. Focused service areas tied to business problems.
3. Proof of capability: real examples, demos clearly labelled as demos, or transparent founder experience.
4. A simple delivery process.
5. Relevant answers to common buyer concerns.
6. Contact details and a secure inquiry form.

Do not invent client logos, testimonials, awards, certifications, or numerical results.

## Technical baseline

- Responsive and accessible layouts.
- Semantic page structure and descriptive page titles.
- Unique meta descriptions where useful.
- Canonical URLs and consistent language/locale handling.
- XML sitemap and correct robots directives.
- HTTPS, secure form validation, spam/rate controls, and privacy-aware analytics.
- Optimized responsive images and efficient asset loading.
- Useful 404 page and monitoring of broken links.
- Structured data only where accurate and supported.

## Content strategy

Write content based on customer questions and real experience. Prioritize useful comparisons, problem diagnosis, implementation guidance, and case studies that explain the problem, approach, trade-offs, and verified result. Avoid generic keyword-stuffed articles.

## Measurement

Track relevant inquiries, qualified leads, source/channel, important page engagement, and search queries. Minimize personal data collected in analytics and honor applicable consent/privacy requirements.

## Launch checklist

- [ ] Domain ownership and DNS confirmed.
- [ ] English/Arabic copy reviewed, if both languages are launched.
- [ ] Contact forms and notification delivery tested.
- [ ] Titles, metadata, sitemap, and robots directives checked.
- [ ] Mobile layout and accessibility basics checked.
- [ ] Performance and broken links checked.
- [ ] Privacy notices and data handling reviewed.
- [ ] Analytics configured with appropriate privacy settings.
- [ ] Backup, deployment, and rollback plan documented.
- [ ] Claims and case studies verified and authorized.
