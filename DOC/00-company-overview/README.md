# Company Overview

## Working identity

**Name:** BAHNES IT SOLUTIONS  
**Brand model:** One company brand with two clear service lines.  
**Service line A:** IT Infrastructure & Security.  
**Service line B:** Software & Digital Solutions.  
**Stage:** Founder-led / early-stage; validate operating capacity before committing to delivery promises.  
**Initial markets:** Egypt and suitable international clients.

Name, legal form, registration, domain, trademark status, and permissions for any regulated or specialized activity must be verified independently.

## Purpose and positioning

Help businesses operate more reliably by combining practical IT infrastructure and security systems with useful websites, applications, and business software.

> **Working positioning:** Integrated IT services, security systems, and digital solutions for businesses.

## The two service lines

### IT Infrastructure & Security
Potential scope: CCTV and surveillance, recording and remote viewing setup, wired/wireless networking, Wi-Fi, routers/switches, business computer setup, IT support and maintenance, and access control/attendance systems when capability, supplier support, and relevant requirements are confirmed.

### Software & Digital Solutions
Potential scope: company websites and e-commerce, web and mobile applications, APIs/integrations, ERP/CRM/POS and inventory/sales workflows, automation, hosting/deployment support, and validated company-owned software products or website templates.

These are planning categories, not a blanket claim that every service is currently staffed, licensed, or ready to sell. Confirm capability, supplier terms, safety/privacy implications, warranty, and support before publishing an offer.

## Business model

1. Win appropriately scoped IT and software work.
2. Deliver, document, hand over, and support it responsibly.
3. Offer recurring maintenance where it solves a real customer need.
4. Record repeated customer problems.
5. Validate potential products/templates before investing substantially.
6. Consider separate legal entities or brands only when a real legal, ownership, commercial, or operational reason emerges.

## Operating principles

- Sell outcomes, scope, and dependable support.
- Use written proposals with deliverables, exclusions, dependencies, and acceptance criteria.
- Protect customer systems, recordings, information, and credentials.
- Do not overpromise installation coverage, warranty, response times, or product availability.
- Prefer maintainable solutions over unnecessary complexity.
- Distinguish collected cash from quotes and invoices.

## Not assumed

A registered entity, approved logo, full-time team, installer certifications, supplier agreements, production website, public case studies, launched SaaS product, or proven recurring revenue are not assumed by this document.
