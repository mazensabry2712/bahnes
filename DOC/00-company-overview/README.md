# Company Overview

## Working identity

**Name:** BAHNES IT SOLUTIONS  
**Business model:** Software services first, with a path to owning and operating proprietary products.  
**Operating stage:** Founder-led / solo, with a low initial budget.  
**Initial markets:** Egypt and international clients.

The company name, legal form, domain, and trademark status must be verified separately. This document does not confirm registration or ownership.

## Purpose

Help growing businesses improve how they operate through dependable software, practical automation, and clear technical delivery.

## Positioning statement

> Business software and digital systems for growing businesses.

Focus on solving a real business problem—not selling a long list of technologies. Potential starting problems include inventory and sales workflows, internal operations, integrations, replacing fragile manual processes, and maintaining existing systems.

## Mission

Deliver understandable, maintainable digital systems that improve customers' day-to-day work and can be supported after launch.

## Long-term vision

Build a trusted software company with recurring service relationships and, only after validating repeated customer needs, one or more company-owned products.

## Operating principles

- Sell outcomes and clear scope, not vague promises.
- Start with discovery and a written proposal.
- Avoid underpricing work that includes uncertain scope or ongoing support.
- Use staged payments and do not treat unpaid invoices as cash.
- Keep customer data and production credentials secure.
- Prefer simple, maintainable solutions over unnecessary complexity.
- Turn repeated client problems into product hypotheses, not automatic product commitments.

## What the company is — and is not

**Today:** A founder-led services business exploring a sustainable niche.  
**Not yet assumed:** A large agency, a fully staffed company, an established SaaS provider, a registered legal entity, or a business with proven recurring revenue.
