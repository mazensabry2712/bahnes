# 01 — Positioning & Service Lines

## Company structure

**Proposed customer-facing model:** one BAHNES IT SOLUTIONS brand with two service lines. Do not create separate companies or websites without a real commercial, legal, ownership, or operational reason.

## Service line A — IT Infrastructure & Security

### CCTV & surveillance
- Requirement consultation and site survey.
- Camera, DVR/NVR, storage, recording, and viewing setup.
- Secure remote viewing where supported.
- Troubleshooting, upgrades, maintenance, and handover documentation.
- Explicit power/network dependencies, warranty, equipment ownership, access to recordings, and privacy responsibilities.

### Networking & infrastructure
- Wired/wireless networking and Wi-Fi.
- Routers, switches, access points, and structured cabling.
- Network planning for business sites.
- Connectivity troubleshooting, documentation, and maintenance.

### Business IT equipment & support
- Workstation, PC, peripheral, and business software setup.
- Fault diagnosis, upgrades, maintenance, and troubleshooting.
- Backup/recovery planning where included.
- Support agreements with defined service hours and response boundaries.

Access control, attendance devices, and other integrations are possible future offerings only after capability, supplier compatibility, lawful use, and any relevant requirements are confirmed.

## Service line B — Software & Digital Solutions

### Websites and commerce
Company websites, service sites, landing pages, e-commerce, catalogs, CMS-managed content, forms, technical SEO, and ongoing improvement.

### Business systems
Inventory, sales, booking, CRM, ERP modules, POS, reporting, integrations, and automation.

### Application engineering
Web/mobile applications, APIs, third-party integrations, data migration, modernization, deployment, maintenance, and consulting where within capability.

### Digital products
Repeatable software products and website templates, developed/sold only after the buyer, license, support, updates, and delivery model are defined.

## Audiences to validate

Potential customers include trading/retail businesses, offices, clinics and service businesses, small/medium companies, and organizations needing both physical IT systems and software. These are hypotheses, not proven segments.

## Service presentation rules

- Lead with business outcomes before technologies.
- Show the two service lines before their detailed services.
- Explain included work, exclusions, dependencies, and when a site visit/discovery is required.
- Separate equipment and recurring third-party fees from labor and support.
- Never promise certifications, coverage, emergency response, warranty, or availability unless verified.
- Use a clear inquiry route that records the customer's service interest.

## Readiness gate

Before publishing an offer, document capability, supplier dependencies, deliverables, exclusions, estimate method, safety/privacy considerations, warranty, support, and handover.
