# 14 — Interactive Demos & Sales Assets

## Interactive System Showcase

A lightweight diagram may show how relevant systems connect—for example sales, inventory, customer records, reporting, networks, and related integrations. It should explain business value rather than display a random technology diagram.

- Static-first and understandable without JavaScript.
- Interactive controls are meaningful and keyboard accessible.
- Use synthetic sample data; never expose customer data.
- Label it as illustrative, not a deployed customer system.
- Do not imply every depicted integration is already offered.
- Build only if it improves comprehension; not a launch dependency.

## Product demos

State what is real or simulated, prerequisites, limitations, and whether data persists. Keep demo environments isolated from production and do not accept sensitive customer data. Use screenshots or a short walkthrough if operating a live demo is risky or costly.

## Template playground

A catalog can use simple categories/filters, desktop/mobile screenshots, and safe live previews. A comparison tool is not needed initially. Never expose private endpoints, credentials, or source files that are not intended for public access.

## Bilingual company profile PDF

After brand and contact details are approved, prepare English and Arabic versions covering company introduction, two service lines, delivery approach, approved work examples or labelled concepts, support offering, and contact details. Keep source files editable, check the exported PDF, and version it. Do not include invented credentials or customer claims.

## Sales kit

Useful reusable assets include discovery questions, IT site assessment checklist, project requirements brief, proposal/quotation outline, handover checklist, and maintenance plan. Prices, equipment, warranties, certifications, and service levels must reflect actual supplier/delivery terms.

Every public demo or downloadable asset needs an owner, purpose, approval status, last-updated date, and clear next contact step.
