# 03 — Brand & Visual System

## Personality and art direction

Premium, clear, capable, engineering-minded, and practical. The experience should feel like a considered technology company rather than a generic AI-looking agency template or gadget shop.

**Proposed direction: Editorial Engineering** — editorial typography and whitespace combined with precise grids, diagrams, strong alignment, and selective technical visuals.

## Proposed palette

These are initial design tokens for testing, not final approved identity assets:
- Midnight Navy: #101C35
- Mint Accent: #55D6BE
- Warm Ivory: #F5F4EF
- Steel Gray: #667085
- White and semantic neutral variants

Define semantic tokens for text, surfaces, borders, focus, success, warning, and error in both themes. Do not rely on color alone to communicate a state.

## Typography

Proposed Latin typeface: Manrope or a comparable readable family.  
Proposed Arabic typeface: IBM Plex Sans Arabic or a comparable Arabic-capable family.

Test hierarchy, weights, Arabic punctuation/numerals, mixed Arabic/English technical terms, line height, mobile rendering, and documents before finalizing.

## Logo brief

- Create a distinctive geometric B monogram suggesting structure, connection, and stability.
- Pair it with a clear BAHNES wordmark and smaller IT SOLUTIONS descriptor.
- Prepare primary, compact, monochrome, light-background, and dark-background variants.
- Test navbar, favicon, social avatar, document, and small-size use.
- Avoid generic camera/chip/cloud symbols, glow, excessive gradients, and fake 3D.
- Keep editable source, export guidance, spacing, and minimum-size notes after approval.

## Layout and media

Use a consistent grid and spacing system, deliberate whitespace, strong alignment, restrained effects, and meaningful images. Use genuine equipment/site photos, software screenshots, technical diagrams, or properly licensed illustrative imagery. Do not present stock assets as customer work or use unrelated photos simply to fill space.

Design light and dark modes as complete systems, not automatic inversion. Keep identity and tagline provisional until explicitly approved.
