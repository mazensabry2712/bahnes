# BAHNES — Company & Website Workspace

**Purpose:** A coordinated workspace to turn the company direction into a practical, reviewable specification. This is planning documentation; it does not claim implementation has started or been verified.

## Direction

BAHNES IT SOLUTIONS is planned as one customer-facing brand with two service lines:

1. **IT Infrastructure & Security:** CCTV/surveillance, networking, business IT setup, support and maintenance, and related systems when capability and requirements are confirmed.
2. **Software & Digital Solutions:** websites, e-commerce, web/mobile apps, APIs/integrations, business systems, automation, and validated software products/templates.

The website has three commercial jobs: generate service inquiries, establish trust through genuine work and useful content, and present a curated catalog of digital products and website templates.

## Working rules

- Keep one canonical source per policy; link to existing DOC sections rather than copying conflicting rules.
- Distinguish agreed planning direction from proposed design, pending decisions, implementation, and verification.
- Never invent clients, testimonials, metrics, certifications, prices, supplier capability, or legal status.
- Validate service capability and support boundaries before publishing an offer.
- Audit the current Laravel scaffold before implementation and preserve Blade server-rendered pages as the planned baseline.
- Build the smallest robust content system that supports the real requirements; avoid over-engineering.
- Every major implementation item needs acceptance criteria and test evidence before it is marked complete.
