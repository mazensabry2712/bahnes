# 02 — Site Map & User Journeys

## Proposed navigation

Home · Services · Our Work · Products · Website Templates · Insights · About · Contact / Start a Project.

Services groups the two service lines and their individual pages. Do not force every service into top-level navigation. Do not fill empty sections with invented content.

## Core pages

- **Home:** clear promise, two service-line entry points, genuine work or labelled demos, short delivery process, ready products/templates only, useful Insights, clear CTA.
- **Services:** overview of both service lines and detailed pages explaining use cases, scope, dependencies, exclusions, FAQs, relevant proof, and next action.
- **Our Work:** genuine projects and case studies with Completed, In Progress, Concept, or Confidential labels. Publish only authorized details.
- **Products:** problem, target buyer, features, limits, screenshots/demo, requirements, license/pricing if approved, support, and actual CTA.
- **Website Templates:** use case, desktop/mobile screenshots, safe live preview, included pages/files, license, price if approved, customization and support.
- **Insights:** reviewed articles with useful internal links and language-specific metadata.
- **About:** company purpose, how the two service lines fit together, and verified company details.
- **Contact / Start a Project:** short general contact form plus a guided brief for substantial projects.

## User journeys

**IT customer:** Home → IT Infrastructure & Security → relevant service → request a site assessment.

**Software customer:** Home → Software & Digital Solutions → service/case study → project brief.

**Product buyer:** catalog → product detail → demo or quote. Show Buy Now only after a real purchase flow exists.

**Template buyer:** catalog/filter → preview → license and scope → purchase request or customization inquiry.

**Research visitor:** Insights article → relevant service/project/product → contact.

## Interaction rules

- Make the main CTA clear without repeating competing CTAs everywhere.
- Preserve the corresponding page when language switching if translated; otherwise use a clear fallback.
- Use breadcrumbs on deeper pages where helpful.
- Support keyboard interaction, mobile forms, readable error states, and useful confirmation.
- Avoid intrusive pop-ups, autoplay media, modal-heavy navigation, and scroll hijacking.
