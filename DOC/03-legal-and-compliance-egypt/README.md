# Legal & Compliance — Egypt

> **Important:** This is an organizational checklist, not legal or tax advice. Rules, portals, fees, forms, and deadlines can change. Verify current requirements with the competent authority and a qualified Egyptian accountant or lawyer before acting.

## Establishment checklist

- [ ] Check the proposed legal/trade name through the current official process.
- [ ] Decide whether to operate as an individual establishment, one-person company, or another suitable legal form after professional advice.
- [ ] Confirm registration steps, documents, address requirements, fees, and current portal workflow.
- [ ] Complete relevant commercial registration and tax registration steps.
- [ ] Open a suitable business bank account when appropriate.
- [ ] Confirm whether VAT registration applies and the relevant threshold and timing.
- [ ] Determine applicable e-invoicing and/or e-receipt obligations.
- [ ] Keep formal records, tax filings, contracts, and receipts.
- [ ] Review employment, subcontractor, intellectual-property, and export-service implications as the business grows.

## Name, domain, and trademark are different

A commercial registration or name-clearance result does not, by itself, confirm that a domain is available or that a trademark is registered. Check each independently. Do not advertise the company as legally registered or trademark-protected until the relevant status is confirmed.

## Client contracts

Before accepting paid work, agree in writing on:
- Parties and authorized signatories.
- Scope, deliverables, exclusions, assumptions, and acceptance criteria.
- Milestones, payment dates, taxes, expenses, and late-payment handling.
- Change requests and the effect on cost or schedule.
- Ownership and licensing of source code, third-party software, and reusable components.
- Confidentiality, data processing, access controls, backups, and incident handling.
- Warranty limits, support hours, service levels, termination, and dispute resolution.

Have a local lawyer adapt templates to the actual business and transaction. A template is not a substitute for reviewed terms.

## Data protection

Customer, employee, website, and end-user data can create legal obligations. Map the data collected, why it is collected, where it is stored, who accesses it, and how long it is retained. Verify the current requirements under Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law and its applicable executive regulations, including any current licensing, registration, transfer, consent, security, or transition requirements.

**Time-sensitive review:** Public legal commentary has reported a 31 October 2026 compliance date in connection with the executive regulations. As the current planning date is 24 October 2026, confirm the exact scope and deadline directly with the competent Egyptian data-protection authority and counsel immediately; do not assume the deadline applies identically to every business.

## Official starting points

- General Authority for Investment and Free Zones (GAFI): https://www.gafi.gov.eg/
- Internal Trade Development Authority (ITDA): https://itda.gov.eg/
- Egypt Tax Authority: https://www.eta.gov.eg/
- Digital Egypt services portal: https://digital.gov.eg/

Use these as starting points and navigate to the current relevant service. Record the check date and save official guidance received.
