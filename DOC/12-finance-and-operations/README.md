# Finance & Operations

## Financial separation

Keep business transactions clearly distinguishable from personal spending. Use a separate account or a disciplined recordkeeping method appropriate to the legal setup, and obtain accounting advice about the correct treatment.

## Cash tracking

Track at minimum:
- Customer, project, invoice, due date, and amount.
- Amount collected and date collected.
- Direct delivery cost and subcontractor cost.
- Software, hosting, payment, and operating expenses.
- Tax amounts or reserves based on professional advice.
- Refunds, write-offs, and unpaid balances.
- Recurring support revenue and obligations.

**Revenue, invoiced amounts, and cash collected are not the same metric.** Plan cash flow around collection dates, not only signed quotes.

## Project cash discipline

- Avoid starting substantial work without the agreed advance or purchase authorization.
- Link later payments to clear milestones.
- Track overdue receivables and follow up politely and consistently.
- Keep a reserve for taxes, refunds, operational expenses, and unexpected problems.
- Do not finance a speculative product using cash needed to finish a customer's paid work.

## Solo-founder capacity

Estimate weekly capacity realistically and reserve time for sales, delivery, administration, learning, and rest. Do not sell more work than can be delivered at an acceptable quality level. Use contractors only when the scope, budget, confidentiality, IP ownership, and quality responsibility are clear.

## Operating routines

### Weekly
- Review cash position and invoices due.
- Check proposals and pipeline next actions.
- Identify delivery risks and customer blockers.
- Confirm next week's priorities.

### Monthly
- Compare expected vs actual project effort and margin.
- Review recurring revenue and support load.
- Check subscription/software costs.
- Update forecast and reserve.
- Decide what to stop, continue, or improve.

## Procurement and subscriptions

Record owner, purpose, renewal date, cost, and cancellation process for every business subscription. Review unused tools regularly. Keep a vendor contact and recovery path for critical services.
